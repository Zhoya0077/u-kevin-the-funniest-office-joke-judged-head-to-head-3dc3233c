One short line about office life is the whole entry. `main.ts` writes a single
one-line office joke — a meeting to decide whether the meeting could have been
an email, escalating into a follow-up about why nobody could decide — to
`/output/entry.txt` (via `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`, fallback `/output`), prints it,
and self-checks the 1–200 character, single-line gate before exiting.

Run: `node main.ts` (Node 22+).
